## Design Context

### Users
College students hosting live quiz rounds and classmates joining from phones. Hosts need to prepare quizzes, manage a room on a projected desktop, and review results. Players need quick, legible, low-friction actions on small screens.

### Brand Personality
Energetic, welcoming, spirited. The product should feel social and competitive without becoming noisy or hard to read.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use the supplied storyboard as the desktop reference: clean screen compositions, confident outlines, clear groupings, and purposeful whitespace. Add a lively but controlled palette to distinguish actions, quiz states, and content. Keep the host/editor surfaces bright and structured; keep live play colorful and high contrast. Preserve phone-first answer controls for players.

### Design Principles
1. Let each screen have one obvious primary action.
2. Use outlines, spacing, and simple frames to make the interface feel sketched into a plan, then use color to make the plan feel alive.
3. Keep host workflows readable at presentation distance and player choices easy to tap.
4. Use color with labels, shapes, and clear text so state is never conveyed by color alone.
5. Preserve responsive layouts, keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
